How this works

Two lines, twenty statements, one source per fact. Side to side is money: higher taxes and more public programs at one end, lower taxes and less spending at the other. Up and down is public safety: more police and enforcement up, police reform and oversight down.

Ward Wise does not endorse candidates. A dot with fewer than 4 facts on a line is pulled toward the middle and drawn faded, so one strong quote cannot push someone to the edge. Words follow one rule: under 1.5 of 10 from the middle reads "near the middle", 1.5 up to 5 reads "somewhat toward", and 5 or more reads "strongly toward". Your answers stay in your browser until you finish; then they are counted, without your name or email, to show how Chicago answered.
